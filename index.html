Hello, I'm Aga

I offer a safe, supportive, and non-judgemental space where you can talk openly about what is happening in your life. Many people come to counselling when they feel overwhelmed, stuck, anxious, or simply need someone to listen and understand. You do not have to face these challenges alone.

My work is grounded in person-centred values, placing you at the heart of the therapeutic process. I believe therapy should feel collaborative, respectful, and tailored to your individual experiences and needs.

As an integrative counsellor, I draw upon a range of therapeutic approaches, allowing me to adapt our sessions to support your unique journey. Together, we can explore your thoughts, feelings, and experiences at a pace that feels right for you.

I offer short-term, medium-term, and open-ended counselling, depending on your needs and what feels most helpful. Sessions are available face-to-face, online via Zoom, or by telephone, providing flexibility and accessibility.

I am a registered member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (MBACP) and work in accordance with their ethical framework. I attend regular clinical supervision, engage in continuing professional development, and hold an Enhanced DBS check.
